## Context

What's the problem? What constraints exist? What forces are at play (technical, organizational, regulatory, cost)?

Keep it short. The reader should know in 5 sentences why we needed to decide something.

## Decision

What did we choose? State it clearly in 1–3 sentences. No hedging.

## Alternatives considered

What did we evaluate and reject? Why? Each alternative gets:

- **Name** — one-line description.
- **Why rejected** — the specific reason this one didn't fit. Be honest; "we didn't have time" is a legitimate reason.

## Consequences

### Positive

- What does this decision enable?

### Negative / costs

- What does this cost us?

### Risks

- What could go wrong because of this decision? How would we know?

### Reversibility

- Is this a one-way door, a reversible decision, or somewhere in between?
- If reversible, what would it take to switch?
